
# TRANSLATION_CONTEXT.md

Version: 1.0
Target Language: English
Target Locale: en-CA, en-US

## 1. Translation Objective

Translate the entire website from Brazilian
Portuguese into natural, professional English.

The translation must preserve the original meaning,
intent, information and communication objectives.

The result must read as if the original content
had been written in English by a proficient
English-speaking professional.

Avoid literal translations and unnatural
sentence structures.

## 2. Target Language

Source language: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)
Target language: English (en-CA) 

Use American English spelling and conventions.

Maintain consistent grammar, punctuation,
vocabulary and writing conventions.

Do not mix American, British and Canadian
English inconsistently.

## 3. Target Audience

The translated content must be accessible to
an international English-speaking audience.

Use language appropriate to the website's
specific audience and subject matter.

Do not assume that every visitor has advanced
technical knowledge.

Adapt technical complexity according to
the original content and its intended audience.

## 4. Tone and Voice

Maintain a professional, clear, natural
and trustworthy communication style.

The writing should be:

- Professional without sounding overly formal.
- Natural rather than mechanically translated.
- Clear and easy to understand.
- Concise without losing relevant information.
- Consistent with the website's identity.

Preserve the original tone and level of formality.

Do not introduce unnecessary marketing language,
exaggerated claims or artificial expressions.

## 5. Translation Principles

### Semantic Fidelity

Preserve the original meaning and communication
intent.

Do not add, remove or invent information.

### Naturalness

Prefer expressions commonly used by native
English speakers.

Restructure sentences when necessary to achieve
natural English.

Do not preserve Portuguese sentence structures
when they sound unnatural in English.

### Contextual Accuracy

Interpret each sentence according to its
page, component, purpose and audience.

Never translate isolated expressions without
considering their context when that context
is available.

### Consistency

Use the same terminology throughout the website.

Consult the project glossary before translating
technical or recurring expressions.

### Cultural Adaptation

Adapt idiomatic expressions and culturally
specific references when necessary.

Do not change factual information or the
original message.

## 6. Grammar and Writing Standards

Apply the following conventions:

- Use correct English grammar and punctuation.
- Prefer clear and direct sentence structures.
- Use active voice when appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary passive constructions.
- Avoid excessive nominalization.
- Maintain consistent capitalization.
- Avoid redundant expressions.
- Use natural transitions between ideas.

## 7. Terminology

Use established English terminology
for the relevant industry or academic field.

Prioritize approved glossary entries.

Preserve proper nouns, brand names and
institutional acronyms unless instructed otherwise.

Do not invent technical equivalents.

Flag terminology that requires human review.

## 8. User Interface Translation

For interface elements:

- Prioritize clarity and usability.
- Use concise button labels.
- Preserve the original action or instruction.
- Keep navigation terminology consistent.
- Adapt text length when interface constraints
  require it, without changing its meaning.

## 9. SEO and Metadata

Translate and localize:

- Page titles.
- Meta descriptions.
- Headings.
- Image alternative text.
- Relevant structured content.

Use natural English keywords rather than
translating Portuguese search terms literally.

Preserve URL structure unless explicitly
authorized to change it.

## 10. Final Quality Standard

Before delivering any translation, verify:

1. Is the meaning faithful to the original?
2. Does the text sound natural in English?
3. Is the terminology consistent?
4. Is the tone appropriate?
5. Is the text grammatically correct?
6. Is the content suitable for its intended audience?
7. Have all technical constraints been respected?

The final result must prioritize accuracy,
naturalness, consistency and readability.
